# Frequently asked questions

## Where is the parking?

It is at 123 Central Avenue, Bishkek, Kyrgyzstan.

## What are the working hours?

The parking is open every day from 06:00 to 23:00.

## How can I pay?

You can pay by card or cash at the entrance after your reservation is approved.

## How far in advance can I reserve?

You can reserve up to 5 days in advance.

## What is the maximum reservation duration?

The maximum duration is 15 hours.

## Can I cancel a reservation?

Yes. You can cancel it before its start time.

## What happens if I arrive late?

Your space is released if you are more than 15 minutes late.

## Is a reservation confirmed immediately?

No. It starts as pending and must be approved by an administrator.

## What if my selected parking type is fully booked?

The request is not sent to the administrator for approval.

## Are there any EV charging parking spaces?

Yes. The parking has 10 EV charging spaces.

## Can I get the Google Map's link for the parking location?

Yes. Open the parking location in Google Maps:
https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=123+Central+Avenue+Bishkek
